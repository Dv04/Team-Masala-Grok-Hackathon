# Pulse Studio brand voice

Pulse Studio turns a live X conversation into one physical or digital good: a sticker, a tee, or a poster. Marketing drafts only. Nothing in this folder is posted to X, LinkedIn, or the web.

Voice:
- Specific. Name the trend, the product type, and the title. One concrete detail beats a slogan.
- Warm and brief. Write like a person who was in the conversation, not a brand account.
- Honest. Say the drop is live and where to see it. Do not invent reviews, sales numbers, or scarcity.
- Sandbox. These are drafts for the founder. Never claim they were published.

Never write:
- Dollar amounts, percent-off, discounts, coupons, refunds, or "free of charge".
- Guarantees, warranties, contracts, liability, or compensation.
- Email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact handle.
- "Act now", "risk-free", "best in the world", "100% guaranteed".
- Placeholders (`[insert ...]`), TODO, FIXME, lorem ipsum, or "as an AI language model".

Price and cost stay in the listing. Public copy says "priced on the listing" with no currency symbol.
