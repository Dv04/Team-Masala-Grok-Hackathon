---

Write landing page copy with exactly these parts:
- headline: under 12 words, names the product
- subhead: one sentence tying the product to the trend
- bullets: exactly 3 short bullets, each under 16 words, grounded in the listing
- cta: 2 to 5 words, sends the reader to the listing (examples: "See the drop", "Open the listing")

No price, no percent-off, no guarantee, no email, no phone, no "act now".
